What Vitsy is for

Vitsy is a personal record and reminder tool. It isn't intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it doesn't work out doses or check how medicines and supplements mix. You use it to note what you take and when, and it reminds you when something is due.

It isn't a substitute for your pharmacist, GP or another health professional, and it doesn't give medical advice.

Medical decisions

Never start, stop or change a medicine or a dose because of anything you see in Vitsy. That includes the estimated level for GLP-1 injections, Patterns and streaks. If something in the app makes you wonder about your treatment, ask your pharmacist, your GP or whoever prescribed it.

Reminders

Reminders depend on your phone: its notification and Focus settings, battery saving and the operating system. We can't promise every reminder will arrive, or arrive on time. Don't rely on Vitsy alone for a medicine where a dose you don't take, or take late, could cause you harm.

Patterns

Patterns are averages worked out from your own check-ins and logs. They show what tended to happen together, not that one thing caused another, and they aren't medical advice.

Emergencies

Vitsy isn't an emergency service and nobody monitors it. In an emergency, call 999 in the UK, 911 in the US, or your local emergency number.

Your records

What you enter is kept on your phone, not by us, so we can't recover it if it's lost. Vitsy's records aren't included in your phone's backups. Use Export your data in Settings to keep your own copy.
