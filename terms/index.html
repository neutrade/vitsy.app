What Vitsy is, and what it isn't

Vitsy is a tool for recording what you take and reminding you when it's due. It is not a medical device and it doesn't give medical advice. It doesn't suggest doses or check how medicines and supplements mix.

Always follow the advice of your pharmacist, GP or other health professional, and don't start, stop or change a medicine because of anything you see in Vitsy. Reminders depend on your phone's settings, such as notifications, Focus and battery saving, so we can't promise every reminder will arrive. Please don't rely on Vitsy alone for a medicine where timing really matters.

Your records

What you enter is kept on your phone, not by us, so we can't recover it if it's lost. Vitsy's records aren't included in your phone's backups. Use Export your data in Settings to keep your own copy.
