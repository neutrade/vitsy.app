Questions

About Vitsy

What is Vitsy?
Vitsy is an app for keeping track of what you take: vitamins, supplements, prescribed tablets and injections, all on one list. It reminds you when something is due, counts down your stock and keeps your history. It's a personal record and reminder tool, made by Neutrade Ltd, a small UK company.
Is Vitsy free?
Yes, for up to 3 items, with no time limit. Vitsy Plus removes the limit and adds Training, Diary check-ins, Patterns, the check-in reminder and firmer reminders. Plus is £29.99 a year after 14 days free, or £4.99 a month. Compare Free and Plus
Can I use Vitsy in the US?
Yes. Vitsy is made for people in the UK and the US, and is coming soon. Prices on this site are UK prices; the App Store shows the price in your currency.
Is Vitsy on Android?
It will be. It's coming to Google Play at the same time as the App Store for iPhone. Register interest and we'll email you when it's out.
Does Vitsy tell me how much to take?
No. Vitsy records what you take and reminds you when it's due. It doesn't suggest doses or check how things mix. For that, ask your pharmacist or GP. Vitsy isn't connected to the NHS.

Your data

Do I need an account?
No. Vitsy works without one, and there's nothing to sign up for.
Where is my data?
On your phone, in an encrypted database. We don't have a copy.
What happens if I lose or change my phone?
Your records stay with that phone. Vitsy's database is left out of iCloud and computer backups, and out of Android's automatic backup, so a new or restored phone won't bring your records with it. To keep a copy, use Export your data in Settings. You can restore a Plus subscription on a new phone.
How do I export or delete my data?
In Settings, under Your data. Export your data gives you spreadsheets and your data file in a ZIP, or a summary PDF to share. Delete all data removes everything Vitsy holds on that phone. It doesn't cancel a Plus subscription; you do that in the App Store or Google Play.
Does Vitsy use Apple Health?
Only if you allow it, and only to read. On iPhone, Vitsy can read your workouts, for Training and for anything timed "After a workout", and your sleep, which the Diary shows but never stores. On iOS 26 or later you can also import the medications you choose. Vitsy never writes anything to Apple Health.

Reminders and injections

Do reminders work without the internet?
Yes. Reminders are scheduled on your phone, not sent from a server, so they arrive without a connection. They do depend on your phone's notification and Focus settings and on battery saving.
Will my medicine names show on my lock screen?
Not by default. Notifications show the stack name, such as "Morning". In Settings, under Names outside the app, you can choose Show names, Stack names only or Hide all names. Prescriptions, injections and anything you mark as discreet are never named, whichever you choose. How reminders keep names private
Can I track injections?
Yes, and it's free. Log where each injection went on a front and back body map that shows when you last used each site. Vitsy doesn't advise on any medicine or on where to inject. How injection sites work

Plus

What happens if I stop paying for Plus?
Nothing you've entered is deleted. Your first 3 items stay free, and what you logged stays readable. Reminders go back to Gentle. Training, new check-ins and Patterns lock until you take Plus again.
How do I cancel Plus?
In the App Store or Google Play, where you subscribed. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play, tap your profile picture, then Payments and subscriptions. Cancel at least a day before a free trial ends and you aren't charged. Deleting Vitsy or its data doesn't cancel a subscription.

If you're struggling

If you're struggling, you don't have to manage on your own.

In the UK

  • Samaritans: call 116 123, free, any time.
  • NHS 111: call 111 for urgent health advice.
  • In an emergency, call 999.

In the US

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call 988 or text 988, free, any time.
  • In an emergency, call 911.

Elsewhere

  • Contact your local emergency services, or find a helpline at findahelpline.com.
