How it works

Add the injection as an item and give it a schedule. Each time you log it, Vitsy asks where it went: pick the site on the body map, front or back, or from a list if you'd rather read words than a picture.

The map then shows the date each site was last used. One site has a ring round it. That's Vitsy's suggestion for next time, picked only because it isn't the site you used last. It isn't advice about where to inject: follow what your nurse, pharmacist or GP showed you.

Logged the wrong site? Recent injections lists your last few, each with Change site, so you can put it right.

Schedules that suit injections

Injections rarely follow an every-day pattern, so Vitsy doesn't assume one. Set every few days, such as every 7 or every 14 days, one day a week, certain weekdays, or as needed. On the day it's due, Today shows the injection as its own card with the time it's due by.

Add your stock and Vitsy counts down what's left and gives you a date to order by, with a reminder on that day if you want one.

Reminders on the day

Injections get a Gentle reminder, with one follow-up if the injection isn't logged. Gentle reminders arrive quietly and don't break through a Focus.

With Plus, injections start on Firm: on iPhone, with Time Sensitive notifications allowed, Firm reminders come through a Focus. Choose Persistent and the follow-up is the last thing to go if your phone's limit on waiting reminders gets tight.

Private by default

Your lock screen never says what you inject. An injection's reminder shows its time, like "09:00 dose", or the name of its stack if you've put it in one. That holds whichever names setting you choose, and widgets and Siri follow the same rule.

There's no account. Your records stay on your phone in an encrypted database, we never receive them, and you can export them or delete them all from Settings.

Who it's for

Anyone who injects on a schedule and wants a record of where each one went: vitamin B12 injections, fertility treatment, GLP-1 medicines, or anything else your prescriber has set. Vitsy logs what you tell it. It doesn't supply, recommend or give advice on any medicine.

What Vitsy doesn't do

  • It doesn't work out doses or tell you how much to inject.
  • It doesn't tell you which site is right for you, or how to inject. Ask your nurse, pharmacist or GP.
  • It doesn't check how medicines mix.
  • It isn't connected to the NHS, a pharmacy or your prescriber.
  • It isn't an emergency service. In an emergency, call 999 in the UK or 911 in the US.

Questions

Can I see when I last used each site?
Yes. The body map shows the date each site was last used, and your most recent injections are listed underneath with their sites.
Does it work for an injection every two weeks?
Yes. Choose Every few days and set it to 14. Vitsy reminds you on the day it's due and shows it on Today.
Is the body map free?
Yes. Injection sites, reminders and stock are all free. Vitsy is free for up to 3 items with no time limit, and an injection counts as one item. Plus removes the limit.
