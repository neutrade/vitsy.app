Stacks: one tap for the lot

A stack is a group of things you take at the same time, such as Morning or With dinner. When you've taken the lot, Take all logs the whole stack. If you only took some, Log some lets you pick which, and Undo puts it right if you tap the wrong thing.

Stacks keep reminders short too: one notification for the stack, like "Morning, 3 items", instead of one for each tub.

Schedules that fit how supplements are taken

Not everything is every day. For each supplement you can choose:

  • every day, or two, three or four times a day
  • certain weekdays, such as Monday to Friday
  • one day a week
  • every few days, such as every other day
  • days on and days off, such as 5 on and 2 off
  • training days only
  • as needed, for things you take only some days

A schedule can start on a date you choose, and end on a date or after a set number of doses if it's a course.

Cycles, worked out for you

Set days on and days off, such as 5 on and 2 off, and Vitsy works out the rest from the start date. On an off day the supplement isn't due, so it doesn't appear on Today and doesn't count against your days on plan.

For gym supplements

Creatine and other gym supplements can follow your training. Choose Training days and the supplement is due only on the days you train: set your usual training days in Settings, or mark a day as training on Today. Or set its timing to After a workout, and Vitsy uses your workouts from Apple Health to say when it's due. Training itself, with a weekly target and your week on Today, comes with Plus.

Stock and reordering

Tell Vitsy how much is in the tub or packet and it counts down as you log, showing about how many days are left and the date to order by. Add how many days delivery takes and the date allows for it. Turn on Remind me to reorder and you get one reminder on that date.

What Vitsy doesn't do

  • There's no product database or barcode scanner: you type in what you take.
  • It doesn't suggest supplements, amounts or timings, or check how things mix.
  • It makes no claims about what any supplement does for you. Your history is your own record.

Questions

Can I take creatine on training days only?
Yes. Choose Training days as its schedule. It's due on the days you train and not on the others.
Can I cycle a supplement, such as 5 days on and 2 off?
Yes. Choose Days on, days off and set both numbers. The cycle counts from the schedule's start date.
Is it free?
Yes, for up to 3 items with no time limit, and stacks, schedules, stock and reminders are all included. Plus removes the item limit.
