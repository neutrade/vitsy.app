Today and the Day Arc

Today opens with the Day Arc: your day drawn as a shallow curve, with a marker for each stack and the sun moving along it to the current minute. In the middle it shows how many you've logged so far.

The sky behind the arc changes as the day goes on. After dark the moon takes over and the card turns to night, so the evening stack is easy to find.

Stacks and Take all

Group the things you take at the same time into stacks, such as Morning or With dinner. When you've taken the lot, one tap on Take all logs the whole stack.

If you only took some, Log some lets you choose which. Tapped by mistake? Undo puts it back.

Reminders that suit what you take

Each item has its own reminder strength: Gentle for supplements, Firm for prescriptions and injections, or Persistent if you want more nudging. On iPhone, turn on Time Sensitive and reminders can come through a Focus.

Item names stay off your lock screen by default. Notifications show the stack name, like "Morning", and you can hide those too.

History, streaks and milestones

History shows the last 12 weeks as a heat map, one square for each day, shaded by how much of that day you logged. A day with nothing logged just says Not logged.

Your streak counts the days you stayed on plan. Reach a milestone, such as 30 days on plan, and you earn a badge to keep. If streaks aren't for you, turn them off.

Diary check-ins and Patterns Plus

Check in from Today or the Diary with how you're feeling: mood and energy out of 5, a few tags, and a note if you want one. Over time, Patterns looks for links between your check-ins and the rest of your day, such as sleep and training, and says so plainly. A pattern isn't proof that one thing causes the other.

Patterns are worked out on your phone and never stored or sent anywhere. New check-ins, Patterns and the check-in reminder come with Plus. Your past check-ins stay readable, and the wellbeing support contacts in the Diary are free for everyone.

Sleep, from Apple Health

On iPhone, the Diary can show your sleep beside each day. Vitsy reads it from Apple Health with your permission, and only reads: it never writes anything to Health.

Your sleep is shown while you look at it and that's all. Vitsy doesn't store it, export it or back it up. Sleep in the Diary is free.

Training Plus

Set a weekly target, say four sessions, and Today shows your week as a row of dots. Workouts come in from Apple Health on iPhone, or you can log a session yourself.

Your training streak counts weeks on target, not days, so a rest day never breaks it.

Private by default

There's no account to make and nothing to sign up for. What you enter is kept on your phone, in an encrypted database.

You choose what shows outside the app, and you can export everything or delete everything from Settings whenever you like. Read the privacy policy.

Apple Watch, widgets and Siri

The Apple Watch app (watchOS 10 or later) shows your next stack with Taken and Skip, and your days on plan. Add a complication, such as the Day Arc, to your watch face.

On your iPhone Home Screen, the "Next stack" widget shows what's due next, with a Log button for the whole stack, and the "History" widget shows your last 12 weeks. With Siri or the Shortcuts app you can log a stack, ask what's next, check whether you've taken everything today, or ask how much of something is left. Say "Vitsy" in the phrase, as in "What's next in Vitsy".

All of this is free. Widgets and the Watch follow your Names outside the app setting, so item names stay hidden by default.

Stock, schedules and injection sites

Tell Vitsy how much you have and it works out the days left and the date to order by. Turn on Remind me to reorder and you get a reminder on that date.

Schedules go beyond every day: set weekdays, every few days or weeks, a day of the month, on and off cycles such as 5 days on and 2 off, a season of the year, training or rest days, or only when needed.

For injections, log the site on a body map that shows when you last used each one. All of this is free.
