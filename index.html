Today and the Day Arc

Your day, drawn as an arc

Today opens with the Day Arc: a shallow curve across your day, with a marker for each stack and the sun moving along it to the current minute. In the middle it shows how many you've logged so far.

The sky behind the arc changes as the day goes on. After dark the moon takes over and the card turns to night, so the evening stack is easy to find.

Stacks and Take all

One tap for the whole stack

Group the things you take at the same time into stacks, such as Morning or With dinner. When you've taken the lot, one tap on Take all logs the whole stack.

If you only took some, Log some lets you choose which. Tapped by mistake? Undo puts it back.

Reminders

Reminders as firm as you need

Each item has its own reminder strength: Gentle for supplements, Firm for prescriptions and injections. For those two only, Persistent keeps its follow-up when your phone's reminder limit gets tight. On iPhone, turn on Time Sensitive and reminders can come through a Focus.

Item names stay off your lock screen by default. Notifications show the stack name, like "Morning", and you can hide those too.

Stock, schedules and injection sites

Know when to reorder, whatever the schedule

Stock and reorder

Tell Vitsy how much you have and it works out the days left and the date to order by. Turn on Remind me to reorder and you get a reminder on that date.

Schedules

Choose every day or several times a day, certain weekdays, one day a week, every few days, days on and days off such as 5 days on and 2 off, training days, or only when needed.

Injection sites

For injections, log the site on a body map that shows when you last used each one.

All of this is free.

History, streaks and milestones

See how it's going

History shows the last 12 weeks as a heat map, one square for each day, shaded by how much of that day you logged. A day with nothing logged just says Not logged.

Your streak counts the days you stayed on plan. Reach a milestone, such as 30 days on plan, and you earn a badge to keep: there are 71 badges (52 free, 19 with Plus). If streaks aren't for you, turn them off.

Also in Vitsy

  • Apple Watch

    The Apple Watch app (watchOS 10 or later) shows your next stack with Taken and Skip, and your days on plan. Add a complication, such as the Day Arc, to your watch face.

  • Home Screen widgets

    The "Next stack" widget shows what's due next, with a Log button for the whole stack. The "History" widget shows your last 12 weeks.

  • Siri and Shortcuts

    Log a stack, ask what's next, check whether you've taken everything today, or ask how much of something is left. Say "Vitsy" in the phrase, as in "What's next in Vitsy".

  • Sleep, from Apple Health

    On iPhone, the Diary can show your sleep beside each day. Vitsy only reads it, with your permission, and never writes anything to Health. It doesn't store, export or back up your sleep.

  • Always free

    Wellbeing support in the Diary, reading your past check-ins and sessions, and workouts from Apple Health never need Plus.

Widgets and the Watch follow your Names outside the app setting, so item names stay hidden by default.

Free to start, Plus when you take more

Your first 3 items are free, with no time limit, and everything above comes with them.

Plus removes the limit and adds Training, Diary check-ins, Patterns and the check-in reminder: 14 days free, then £29.99 a year, or £4.99 a month with no free days.

Patterns looks for links between your check-ins and the rest of your day, such as sleep and training. A pattern isn't proof that one thing causes the other.

Questions

Does Vitsy tell me what to take?
No. Vitsy records what you take and reminds you when it's due. It doesn't suggest doses or check how things mix. For that, ask your pharmacist or GP. Vitsy isn't connected to the NHS.
What if I stop paying for Plus?
Nothing you've entered is deleted. Your first 3 items stay free, and what you logged stays readable. Training, new check-ins and Patterns lock until you take Plus again.
