Training

Set a weekly target, say four sessions, and Today shows your week as a row of dots that fill in as you train. Workouts from Apple Health count on their own. You can also log a session yourself, or tap I've just trained as you finish.

Add how hard a session was, how it felt and a short note, so you can look back at your weeks in History. Your training streak counts weeks on target, not days, so a rest day never breaks it.

Check-ins and Patterns

A check-in takes a few seconds: mood and energy out of 5, any tags that fit, and a note if you want one. You can check in from Today or the Diary.

Over time, Patterns looks for links between how you felt and the rest of your day, such as how long you slept or whether you trained, and says what it found in plain words. A pattern isn't proof that one thing causes the other. Patterns are worked out on your phone and never stored or sent anywhere.

The check-in reminder: pick a morning time, an evening time or both, and Vitsy reminds you to check in. If you've already checked in for that part of the day, it stays quiet.

Questions

What happens when the 14 free days end?
Plus renews automatically at £29.99 a year until you cancel it. You cancel in the App Store or Google Play, where you subscribed. Cancel at least a day before the trial ends and you aren't charged.
What if I stop paying for Plus?
Nothing you've entered is deleted. Your first 3 items stay free, and what you logged stays readable. Training, new check-ins and Patterns lock until you take Plus again.
Does Vitsy tell me what to take?
No. Vitsy records what you take and reminds you when it's due. It doesn't suggest doses or check how things mix. For that, ask your pharmacist or GP.
Where is my data?
On your phone, in an encrypted database. There's no account, and we don't have a copy.
